About

Professional posture care software with a calm clinical foundation.

ALIGNMENT+ organizes patient-facing posture correction workflows so future exercise, tracking, and analysis modules can be added cleanly.

Mission

Make posture and movement support easier to understand, safer to follow, and more consistent between care sessions.

Vision

A rehabilitation platform where users, clinicians, and support tools share one accessible pathway.

Why ALIGNMENT+

The app avoids fitness-first visuals and focuses on reassurance, education, and structured selection.

Technology used

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, responsive components, reusable utilities, and browser local storage for prototype state.

Future roadmap

Exercise plan expansion

Condition-specific plans and exercise details.

Routine and progress tracking

Completion history, clinician notes, and daily summaries.

AI posture analysis

Camera-assisted posture observations after safety and privacy review.
