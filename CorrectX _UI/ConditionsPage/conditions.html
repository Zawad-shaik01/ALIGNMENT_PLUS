Spinal Curvature Disorders

Conditions related to abnormal spinal curves and posture imbalance.

Spinal

Scoliosis

Sideways curvature of the spine that may affect posture and body symmetry.

Spinal

Kyphosis

Excessive outward curve of the upper spine, often causing rounded back posture.

Spinal

Lordosis

Excessive inward curve of the lower spine, often creating swayback posture.

Pelvic Alignment Disorders

Conditions related to pelvic tilt, hip level imbalance, and postural foundation.

Pelvic

Anterior Pelvic Tilt

Forward tilt of the pelvis that may increase lower back arch and muscle imbalance.

Pelvic

Posterior Pelvic Tilt

Backward tilt of the pelvis that may flatten the lower back posture.

Pelvic

Pelvic Obliquity

Uneven hip height where one side of the pelvis sits higher than the other.

Lower Body Conditions

Conditions affecting knees, feet, ankles, gait, and movement balance.

Lower Body

Knock Knees

Knees angle inward and may touch while the ankles remain apart.

Lower Body

Bow Legs

Legs curve outward, creating a gap between the knees when standing.

Lower Body

Flat Feet

Low or absent foot arch that can affect walking, posture, and balance.

Lower Body

High Arch

Excessively high foot arch that may reduce shock absorption during movement.

Lower Body

Hallux Valgus

Big toe deviation that may form a bunion and affect walking comfort.

Lower Body

Ankle Instability

Weak or unstable ankles that may lead to repeated sprains or poor balance.

Lower Body

Abnormal Gait

Altered walking pattern such as toe walking or improper foot strike.

Lower Body

Poor Balance

Reduced stability, coordination, and control during standing or movement.
