About ALIGNMENT+

ALIGNMENT+ is a corrective exercise and posture support platform designed to help users follow structured routines for physical alignment improvement. The system combines exercise guidance, corrective device support, doctor feedback tracking, and progress monitoring in one place.

Medical Safety Notice

This system is not a medical diagnosis tool. Users must consult a qualified medical professional before starting any corrective exercise routine.
